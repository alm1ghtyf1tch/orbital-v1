# Earth imagery

`earth-blue-marble.jpg` is NASA's **Blue Marble: Land Surface, Ocean Color and Sea Ice** texture map (2048 × 1024 pixels), downloaded from:

https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57730/land_ocean_ice_2048.jpg

Credit: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble imagery. This is a historical composite used as a geographic basemap, not current weather imagery.

NASA media usage guidelines permit informational uses, including computer graphical simulations and internet pages, with acknowledgement and without implying endorsement:

https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The application uses no NASA insignia or endorsement. Texture files are bundled locally so the globe works without a third-party image server. Lighting and atmospheric glow are illustrative visual effects; they do not determine satellite illumination or optical visibility.
